About RM Designs

RM Designs is part of R&M Group AB. We build small, family-friendly apps with a pastel aesthetic and a focus on clarity, warmth and accessibility.

Our process is simple: design carefully, ship early, iterate often. We listen to user feedback and add improvements in small, meaningful steps.

Mini Memories is our first app in this direction: a private space for families to collect memories that matter.

Principles

  • Delight: Friendly visuals, soft pastels, and clear typography.
  • Privacy: Simple policies, transparent choices, and control over your data.
  • Care: Features that actually help, delivered in small, polished steps.
