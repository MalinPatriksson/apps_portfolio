Delete Data – Pet Memories+

Last updated: 2025-09-01 • Developer: R&M Group AB • Contact: info@rmdesigns.se

This page explains how you can delete specific items from your account (without deleting the entire account). If you want to delete your whole account, see Delete Account.

Delete a profile (pet profile)

  • Open the profile, tap the ⋯ menu, select Delete, and confirm.
  • Alternative: Profile picture → Delete profile → confirm.

Delete an album

  • Open the album, tap the ⋯ menu, select Delete, and confirm.

Delete an event (photo/memory)

  • Open the event → Edit → press and hold “Hold to delete” → confirm.

What happens: The selected item is permanently removed from your account. Deleted items are not kept as a recoverable copy.

Can’t access the app?

Email us from your registered address with subject “Delete Pet Memories data” at info@rmdesigns.se. We verify ownership and process within 30 days.

Radera data – Pet Memories+

Senast uppdaterad: 2025-09-01 • Utvecklare: R&M Group AB • Kontakt: info@rmdesigns.se

Här förklarar vi hur du tar bort enskilda saker (utan att radera hela kontot). För kontoradering, se Ta bort konto.

Ta bort profil (djurprofil)

  • Öppna profilen, tryck på ⋯-menyn, välj Ta bort och bekräfta.

Ta bort album

  • Öppna albumet, tryck på ⋯-menyn, välj Ta bort och bekräfta.

Ta bort händelse (foto/minne)

  • Öppna händelsen → Redigera → håll in ”Håll inne för att radera” → bekräfta.

Det som händer: Det valda objektet tas bort permanent. Ingen återställningsbar kopia sparas.
