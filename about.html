About RM Designs

RM Designs is part of R&M Group AB, a small Swedish studio creating family-friendly apps with a pastel aesthetic. Our focus is on clarity, warmth, and accessibility — apps that feel welcoming, safe and joyful to use.

We believe technology doesn’t have to be complicated or overwhelming. Our process is simple: design carefully, ship early, and improve continuously based on user feedback. Every step is about delivering features that truly help, in a polished and thoughtful way.

Our apps

Mini Memories — a private space for families to collect and cherish the moments that matter most. From everyday joys to big milestones, Mini makes it easy to save, organise, and relive memories together.

Pet Memories — built with the same spirit, but for the furry (or feathery) members of the family. A safe and loving space to keep track of every pawprint, cuddle, and chapter of your pet’s life.

Principles

  • Delight: Friendly visuals, soft pastels, and clear typography.
  • Privacy: Transparent policies and full control over your data.
  • Care: Features that genuinely support families and pet owners, delivered in small, meaningful steps.
