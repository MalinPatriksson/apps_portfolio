Take control of your money

Spendly gives you a clear overview of your finances in one place. See your income, expenses, and what is actually left, so you can make decisions with confidence instead of guessing.

Plan your monthly budget

Track income, expenses, and savings month by month. Whether you want structure or flexibility, Spendly helps you stay aware of your money without feeling restricted.

Organize every expense

Create categories and subcategories that match your real life. From rent and groceries to self care and fun, everything has its place and stays easy to understand.

One app. Multiple budgets

Keep different budgets for different parts of your life. Monthly finances, shared household budgets, trips, or savings goals all live side by side in one app.

Shared budgets made simple

Plan together with your partner or family. See incomes, expenses, and remaining balance in real time so everyone stays on the same page.

Invite in seconds

Share a budget with a simple code. No accounts to manage, no complicated setup. Just invite and start planning together right away.

See who is part of your budget

Stay in control of shared budgets by seeing exactly who has access. Add or remove participants easily whenever your situation changes.

Made for everyone

Use your language and your currency. Spendly adapts to you, not the other way around, making budgeting feel simple and familiar wherever you are.
