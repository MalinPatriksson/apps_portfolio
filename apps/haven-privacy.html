Privacy Policy / Integritetspolicy

Effective: 2026-02-02 • Developer: R&M Group AB • Contact: info@rmdesigns.se

English

This Privacy Policy applies to Haven, developed by R&M Group AB. Haven provides emotional support and self-reflection tools. It is not medical advice and does not replace professional help.

Information Collection and Use

User Provided: Haven may process the content you enter, such as chat messages, mood logs, tasks, and preferences. This is used to personalize your experience, prompts, and notifications.

App Memory: Haven stores a limited chat history and a short summary to improve future responses and reminders.

Automatically Collected: We do not collect app usage analytics or crash reports in the app.

Notifications

If you enable notifications, the operating system may create a notification token to deliver reminders.

Third-Party Services

Haven uses third-party services to deliver core functionality. Chat is processed via our server on Firebase (Google Cloud Functions), and messages are forwarded to an AI provider to generate responses. We also use Firebase (Google) for backend services and push notifications. We do not use third-party analytics in the app.

Data Storage and Retention

Data is stored locally on your device and remains there until you delete the app or clear it within the app.

Children

Haven is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

AI Content and Responsibility

Haven is not medical advice and should not be used in urgent situations. In an emergency, call 112.

Security

We implement reasonable measures to protect your information, but no system is completely secure. Please protect your device and account.

Changes

We may update this policy. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.

Svenska

Insamling och användning

Användarinformation: Haven kan behandla innehåll du skriver in, såsom chattmeddelanden, humörloggar, tasks och preferenser. Det används för att anpassa upplevelsen, prompts och notiser.

Notiser
